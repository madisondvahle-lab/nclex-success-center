`PASS`, `MINOR`, and `MAJOR` describe the audit disposition, not clinical
severity. A `MAJOR` item may still have a clinically sound current key; it
means the question has an important construction, overlap, or course-alignment
issue requiring revision or instructor confirmation. No item is recommended
for deletion. Several foundational questions should remain foundational.

approval, the entire bank should be recalculated for answer position, answer
set, option length, difficulty, and overlap.
