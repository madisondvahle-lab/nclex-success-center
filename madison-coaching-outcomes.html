NCLEX Coaching Outcomes

Actual NCLEX outcomes after verified paid coaching. Student reports are included in the known-outcome rate, but are never labeled independently verified.

Report filters

Date filters use the first post-coaching examination, not purchase date. Untested students remain listed separately outside the date cohort. Filters never substitute a later attempt. Package cohorts can overlap; do not add them together.

Controlled de-identified reports, not public downloads: exact dates may permit identification. Names, private notes, document links, payment references, and raw student IDs are excluded.

Outcomes overview

Known-outcome pass rate = known passes / (known passes + known failures), including explicit student reports. Unverified results and unresolved histories do not enter that denominator. Verification coverage = documentation-supported or independently checked known outcomes / all eligible tested students.

    Paid NCLEX coaching purchases

    Record only NCLEX coaching/tutoring purchases. A standalone Strategy Session, free consultation, or academic tutoring does not qualify. Payment references are manually reviewed; a booking or package-purchased marker is not payment verification.

    Completed paid NCLEX coaching sessions

    Actual NCLEX attempts

    Reuses existing examination records and preserves CPRs. Attempt number is the student's lifetime NCLEX attempt number, not session count. Unknown dates and same-day coaching/exams need review; no attempt is silently assumed to be the first.

    Verification and private evidence

    Nursys or a licensing-board finding supports licensure, but not necessarily a particular examination date or attempt. Only mark it as supporting this attempt after matching identity, RN/PN credential, and separate information establishing the attempt. No license found never means failure.

    PDF, PNG, JPEG, WebP; up to 10 MB. Files and evidence metadata are versioned, not overwritten. Save a verification copy only where permitted. Student reports remain student-reported even when a file is attached.
