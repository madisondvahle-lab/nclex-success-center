Consultation tool

Consult diagnostic.

Up to 30 items, exam-style snapshot to run with a new prospective student while you share your screen on Zoom. It mixes single-answer, select-all-that-apply, dosage-calculation, and a six-item case study, then shows where the gaps look like content and where they look like how the question is asked.

Questions are spread evenly across content areas. The 6-item case study is included at 20 or more.

Running it on Zoom

  • Share this browser window, then press Start. The exam fills the screen like the real testing interface.
  • Have the student tell you their answer, or hand over remote control. Tell them to right-click an option to cross it out, just like the exam.
  • Use Feedback in the toolbar to jot a note about how they reasoned on any item; it appears in the results.
  • Choose a consultation to save results privately to that student's record (admin-only). With none selected, results stay on this page only.
