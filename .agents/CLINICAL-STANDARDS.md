# Clinical & NCLEX Content Standards

## Default rule: clinical content is locked
Clinical content is LOCKED unless the assigned task explicitly authorizes clinical-content editing.

Locked content includes:
- question stems
- answer choices
- correct answers
- rationales
- clinical facts and teaching points
- medication information
- laboratory values
- question IDs
- NGN case-study clinical data

A technical, UI, security, navigation, scoring-engine, or refactoring task does not authorize clinical edits.

## When clinical editing is authorized
Prioritize:
1. patient safety
2. current evidence and accepted clinical guidance
3. NCLEX-RN relevance
4. clinical judgment over trivia
5. realistic nursing scope, prioritization, delegation, and escalation

The intended learners are nursing graduates preparing for NCLEX-RN. Avoid replacing useful clinical judgment with low-value introductory material merely to simplify content.

Do not make a question easier simply because the concept is complex.

## Question-bank integrity
Never silently:
- change the keyed answer
- reuse an existing question ID for a different question
- introduce duplicate items
- remove rationales to simplify code
- change question wording during unrelated refactoring
- expose answer keys to students before submission

After authorized question-bank changes, validate as applicable:
- unique IDs
- valid answer indexes/keys
- required question structure
- scoring behavior
- rationale mapping
- CAT/blueprint compatibility
- NGN case integrity

## NCLEX and CAT claims
Do not describe the Success Center diagnostic or mock CAT as the official NCLEX CAT, an official NCLEX score, a certification of competence, or a guarantee of passing.

Use accurate educational language such as readiness estimate, mock CAT, diagnostic, practice assessment, or personalized practice when appropriate.

## Scope discipline
If a clinical issue is discovered during an unrelated technical task, document it for review rather than silently changing it unless the task explicitly authorizes the clinical correction.
